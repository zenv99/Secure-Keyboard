# Secure Keyboard privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

**Secure Keyboard collects no data. Nothing you type, say or copy leaves your device.**

## What the app does with your data

- **Typed text.** Used on the device only, to suggest and correct words. Words and typing habits the keyboard learns are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. You can erase them at any time in Settings > Text correction > Wipe everything learned. Password and no-learning fields are not learned from.
- **Voice.** When you hold the dictation button, the microphone records audio that is turned into text on the phone by the Whisper model built into the app. The audio is held in memory while it is processed and is not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Clipboard history.** If you turn it on, it is stored on the device only.

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over a network.
- It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, accounts or remote configuration.
- It does not share data with anyone, because it holds no data outside your phone.
- Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are turned off.

## Permissions

- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** only while you hold the dictation button.
- **Vibrate:** key and dictation feedback.
- **Receive boot completed:** lets the keyboard restore its state after the phone restarts.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Open source and audits

The full source code is public at https://github.com/zenv99/Secure-Keyboard so you can check these statements. The app has not yet been independently audited.
