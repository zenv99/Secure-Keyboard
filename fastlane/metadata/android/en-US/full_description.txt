Secure Keyboard is an Android keyboard forked from HeliBoard. It is not the official HeliBoard.

The app does not declare an INTERNET permission. CI rejects a build that adds one, or that adds a permission or exported component outside the allowlist. Android backup and device transfer are disabled. There is no account or analytics SDK.

Dictation uses the microphone only while the mic key is held. Speech is transcribed on the device with whisper.cpp. The model checksum is pinned. Learned words can be wiped. Password fields and fields that disallow personalized learning are not learned from. Clipboard history is off unless you enable it.

Text replacement, a space-bar trackpad, autocorrect, and swipe typing run on the device.

GPL-3.0. Source: github.com/zenv99/Secure-Keyboard.

Not independently audited. GrapheneOS hardened_malloc and the per-app network toggle were not confirmed on a GrapheneOS phone. See SECURITY.md in the repository.
