Secure Keyboard is a private keyboard that works the way you expect, and keeps everything on your phone.

The app has no internet permission. It cannot send what you type or say anywhere. No ads, no tracking, no accounts.

Features:
• Learning autocorrect that fixes slips, including neighbouring-key mistakes, and learns how you type. Wipe what it has learned at any time.
• Swipe typing: slide across the letters to type a word.
• Push-to-talk dictation: hold the mic and speak. Speech is converted to text on the device. No audio leaves your phone.
• Smooth cursor control: drag on the space bar to move the cursor in any direction.
• Emoji suggestions as you type.
• Clean layout with a single emoji key and a large send key.
• Themes, layouts, clipboard history, one-handed mode and more.

Open source (GPL-3.0). Source code: github.com/zenv99/Secure-Keyboard. Based on HeliBoard. This is not the official HeliBoard.

Not yet independently audited.
